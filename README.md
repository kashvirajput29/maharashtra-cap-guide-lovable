## 📌 Problem Statement

First-year engineering CAP applicants in Maharashtra routinely face scattered, unverified, and misleading information regarding engineering colleges, cutoffs, and post-CAP institutional rounds. Sifting through commercial counseling portals, sponsored social media content, and outdated forums causes unnecessary confusion, heightened anxiety, and costly errors during the official option form submission.

---

## 💡 Solution

**Maharashtra CAP Guide** bridges this gap by centralizing verified data for **50 engineering colleges across Maharashtra**. Every entry is strictly mapped to authoritative sources:
- **Maharashtra State CET Cell FE Engineering Portal**
- **Official college portals & circulars**
- **Accredited institutional indicators** (NAAC, NIRF, NBA, and Government/Aided status)

The platform avoids synthetic rankings or speculative tier lists, maintaining an honest, transparent approach to data presentation.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Verified College Profiles:** Comprehensive details covering city, district, university affiliation, engineering branches, verified accreditations, and direct source links.
- **CAP Cutoffs:** Real cutoff metrics displayed with clear, transparent *"Not published yet"* placeholders when official figures have not been released.
- **Post-CAP & ACAP/ILS Tracking:** Live status tracking and direct links to official admission circulars for Against CAP (ACAP) and Institute Level Seats (ILS).
- **College Matchmaker:** Filter options by percentile, reservation category, and branch preferences to categorize institutions into **Safe**, **Moderate**, and **Ambitious** buckets.
- **Scenario Comparison:** Test hypothetical score and category variations to analyze how shifts impact admission possibilities.
- **Option Form Generator:** Select, organize, and reorder shortlisted colleges to export a printable reference sheet formatted for CAP option entry.

All MHT-CET / JEE Main cutoff percentiles, DTE institute codes, branch intake capacities, and category-wise seat matrices displayed in this application are compiled from publicly available historical CAP round allotment records.
